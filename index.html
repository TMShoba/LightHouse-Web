Questions we get asked

If yours isn't here, it's faster to just ask — see the contact details below.

How long does a website actually take?

Starter sites take about two weeks, Business sites about four, and Custom Builds depend on scope — we'll give you a real date after the Discover call, not before.

Do I own the website afterward?

Yes. Hosting and domain accounts are set up in your name, and you receive full files and admin access at handover.

Can you work with a logo and branding we already have?

Yes — we design around your existing brand rather than replacing it. If you don't have branding yet, we can scope that separately.

What if I need changes after launch?

The first 30 days of fixes are included. After that, we offer a simple monthly care plan, or you can update content yourself using the handover guide.
